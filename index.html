Good to know

The stuff people usually call to ask.

Answered here so you can book with confidence — and the phone stays open for the questions that really need a human.

Do I need a motorcycle license?
No — a regular, valid driver's license is all you need. Just show it to your driver at delivery.
Who can ride?
Riders must be 18 or older with a valid driver's license, wearing closed-toe shoes, and comfortable operating a moped. For safety, we're not able to rent to riders who are pregnant.
Do I need riding experience?
A little comfort on two wheels helps, but no license or prior moped experience is required. Your driver walks you through the controls and makes sure you're confident before you head out.
Is there a deposit?
Yes — a refundable deposit is held during your rental and returned once the mopeds are back in good shape.
Where do you deliver?
Across Door County — just enter your delivery address at checkout, whether that's a rental, a restaurant, or the beach where your day starts.
What if it rains?
Door County weather can shift fast. If your rental day looks rough, get in touch and we'll work out the best option with you.
Are helmets included?
Yes — just add each rider's helmet size at checkout so we bring the right fit.
How long can I keep it?
Anything from a half day up to multiple days. Every day past your first 32 hours is just $100, so longer trips get cheaper by the day.
What should I bring?
A windbreaker or light jacket, sunglasses, sunscreen, a water bottle, a non-perishable snack, and closed-toe shoes. We handle the rest.
